## How to be better founders

1. **Talk to students every week.** At least 3 conversations a week each. Most startup mistakes come from guessing what customers want.
2. **Ship small and fast.** A webinar this week beats a perfect course in three months.
3. **Write a founders' agreement now, while things are easy.** Roles, equity split, vesting (for example over 4 years), what happens if one person steps back, and who owns the course content. This protects both the company and your relationship. A template from your incubator or a startup lawyer is enough at the start.
4. **Keep work and personal time separate.** Have a fixed weekly "founder meeting" with an agenda, so startup discussions don't take over everything else.
5. **Use a decision log.** One line per decision: what, why, date. It stops the same debate happening twice.
6. **Track five numbers.** Signups, paid students, completion rate, student results (reports, interviews, offers) and money in vs money out.
7. **Protect research time.** Your PhD work is your credibility. Keep the startup to fixed hours and cap cohort sizes until you have TAs.
8. **Get a mentor.** Your institute's incubator or a founder who has built an edtech company can save you months.
