# Niche markets and pricing

Written 10 November 2026. Prices are starting guesses to test with real students, not facts.

## Why a niche

A course for "everyone who wants AI" competes with Scaler, upGrad and every YouTube channel. A course for a specific student with a specific goal sells itself, because that student sees it is made for them. Win one niche, then add the next.

## The niches, in order

### 1. Indian-document AI (your strongest niche)
- **Who:** students who want to work in AI for banking, fintech, insurance, govtech or legal tech.
- **Why it sells:** Indian companies need OCR and document AI that work on real Indian documents (multiple scripts, poor scans, mixed layouts). Generic OCR often fails on them, as KYC providers themselves point out. Few courses teach this, and it is exactly your research area.
- **Course angle:** "Build document AI for India: OCR, verification and extraction on Indian-language documents."

### 2. Research-internship applicants
- **Who:** 2nd and 3rd year students applying to SRIP (IIT Gandhinagar), the IIT Mandi summer internship, GRISHMA (IIT Kharagpur), RISE UP (IIT Jammu), ACM iKDD Uplink, and international programs like MITACS Globalink and DAAD WISE.
- **Why it sells:** clear goal, clear deadline. Many application windows fall in winter and spring, so run a cohort that finishes before them (check each program's dates every year).
- **Course angle:** "Build a research profile in 8 weeks before internship season."

### 3. MS and PhD aspirants
- **Who:** students who want MS or PhD admission in India or abroad.
- **Why it sells:** admissions committees look for research experience and recommendation letters. A real project with mentor feedback helps both.
- **Course angle:** "Your first research project, with a paper draft and talk to show."

### 4. GATE Data Science and AI (DA) aspirants
- **Who:** students preparing for the GATE DA paper.
- **Why it sells:** they already study linear algebra, probability and ML theory. You add the hands-on projects that make the theory stick. This also fits Shikhar's own GATE prep.
- **Course angle:** "GATE DA theory, applied: the maths behind ML with real projects."

### 5. Final-year project students (via colleges)
- **Who:** final-year BTech students who must do a major project, often with little guidance.
- **Why it sells:** large volume; colleges want better projects and may pay for structured guidance.
- **Course angle:** sold to colleges as a "guided research project program" for a batch.

**Suggested order:** start with niche 1 and 2 together (one Document AI Research Track timed before internship season). Add 3 and 4 in the second half of the year. Approach colleges for 5 once you have student results to show.

## Pricing ladder

| Level | Product | Price (test) | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | YouTube, webinars, "How to read a paper" guide | Free | Build trust and an email/WhatsApp list |
| Starter | Paper-reading bootcamp or mini course (recorded, 1 to 2 weeks) | ₹499 to ₹999 | First purchase, low risk for students |
| Core | Pilot cohort (6 weeks, live, team project) | ₹2,999 to ₹4,999 | Main product in year one |
| Flagship | Research Track (8 to 10 weeks, mentor reviews, paper and talk, application kit) | ₹9,999 to ₹14,999 | Once you have pilot results |
| Premium | Research Track Pro (one-on-one mentoring, recommendation letter where earned, submission support) | ₹24,999 to ₹39,999 | Small number of seats |
| Add-ons | CV review, mock research interview, paper review | ₹499 to ₹1,499 | Extra income, low effort |
| B2B | College program for a batch, or a 2-day workshop | Per batch or per workshop | Volume and steady income |

**Pricing tips**
- Offer an early-bird price and a no-cost EMI option through your payment provider for the higher tiers.
- Give a few need-based scholarships per cohort. It builds goodwill and good stories.
- Never price on a promise of placement. Price on what students build and the support they get.
- Raise prices only after each cohort shows better results than the last.
