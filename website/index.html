Curriculum · pilot

Six weeks from zero research to a finished project.

Every week ends with something you can see: a running baseline, a results table, a draft, a talk.

OCR on Hindi and multi-script government forms
Detecting tampered or edited documents
Extracting tables from scanned documents

Pilot outline. Topics may shift a little as we learn from the first cohort.

WEEK 1

Research foundations
  • Python and PyTorch refresher in Colab
  • Git and GitHub, Hugging Face basics
  • How to read a research paper, and how research questions are chosen
  • Teams formed, project problems assigned

OutputTeam repo set up and one paper summarised

WEEK 2

The problem and a baseline
  • Public and licensed datasets only, and why that matters for identity documents
  • Run classic OCR baselines (Tesseract, PaddleOCR)
  • Evaluation: character and word error rates, honest test splits

OutputA working baseline with numbers on the shared leaderboard

WEEK 3

Modern document AI models
  • Transformer OCR and document models: TrOCR, Donut, LayoutLM-style models
  • Fine-tuning on your dataset with Hugging Face
  • Reading the results, not just the score

OutputA model that beats your baseline, or a clear reason why it doesn't

WEEK 4

Improve, then ablate
  • Try one or two real improvements
  • Ablations: which change actually helped
  • Error analysis with example failures

OutputA results table and figures you can defend

WEEK 5

Write and present
  • Write a 4 to 6 page report in Overleaf, section by section
  • Clean figures with matplotlib
  • Turn the paper into a 5-minute talk

OutputReport draft reviewed by your mentor

WEEK 6

Demo day and applications
  • Demo day: present your work
  • Research CV, GitHub cleanup, emails to professors
  • Mock research interviews

OutputFinal report, recorded talk and an application kit

How we teach

We teach research the way it really works.

Reading papers, setting baselines, running ablations, writing and presenting. Small groups, real feedback, and no shortcuts.

Live weekly class

One focused session a week, recorded so you can rewatch it.

Small teams

Work in a team of 2 to 3. Cohorts stay small so every team gets attention.

Weekly mentor review

A researcher reviews your code, results and writing every week, and tells you what to fix.

Starter code that runs

Template repos and Colab notebooks, so you spend time on research, not setup.

Shared leaderboard

Common evaluation scripts, so every team's numbers are honest and comparable.

Community

A Discord for questions between classes, plus demo days and talks.

FAQ

Questions students ask us.

Do I need research experience?

No. You need basic Python. Week 1 covers PyTorch, Git, Hugging Face and how to read a paper.

Will this guarantee me an internship or a job?

No, and be careful of anyone who promises that. We prepare you to compete: real work to show, a strong research CV and practice for interviews. The result depends on your work and the programs you apply to.

Is the course online?

Yes. Classes are live online, recorded for rewatching, with a Discord community between classes.

Is Leapverse part of an IIT?

No. Leapverse is an independent venture. Our founders are researchers at IIT, and we teach from that experience.

Who owns my project?

You and your team do. You write the code and the report yourselves, and keep everything in your own public GitHub repository.

Can my college run this for a whole batch?

Yes. We run guided research-project programs for final-year batches and 2-day AI research workshops. Use the form below and pick "My college".
