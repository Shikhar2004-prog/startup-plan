Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy explains what the Leapverse website and Android app collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • When you apply or contact us: your name, email, WhatsApp number, college, year of study, interest and any message you write.
  • Nothing else. We do not use advertising trackers, and the app does not ask for access to your camera, contacts, location or files.

How we use it

  • To reply to you about courses, cohort dates and pricing.
  • To run the course you join (class invites, project updates).

We never sell your information. We share it only with the tools we use to run courses (for example email and form services), and only as needed.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep application details for as long as you are interested in our courses. Ask us at any time and we will delete them.

Children

Leapverse courses are for college students. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Contact

Email us at the address listed on our website.
